## 4. Typography System

* **Primary Serif (Headlines & Title Words):**  
  *Recommended:* **Playfair Display**, **Newsreader**, or **Georgia / Times New Roman**  
  *Usage:* Hero titles, chapter rubrics, logo wordmark, pull quotes.
* **Secondary Monospace (Metadata & System Specs):**  
  *Recommended:* **Courier New**, **JetBrains Mono**, or **SF Mono**  
  *Usage:* Archive numbers, word counts, calculator ledgers, status indicators.
* **Clean Sans-Serif (Body & Form Fields):**  
  *Recommended:* **Geist**, **Inter**, or **system-ui**  
  *Usage:* Long-form explanatory paragraphs, navigation links, buttons, inputs.

## 5. Usage Rules (Do's and Don'ts)

* **DO** use the circular avatar `wayforth-avatar-circle.svg` for WhatsApp Business to ensure the "WAYFORTH" text and monogram remain perfectly inside the circular profile crop.
* **DO** maintain ample white space around the horizontal wordmarks.
* **DO NOT** stretch, distort, or alter the proportions of the upward arrow vertex inside the monogram.
* **DO NOT** place the dark navy wordmark on dark navy backgrounds (use `wayforth-logo-light.svg` instead).
* **DO NOT** add drop shadows or gradients to the logo marks.
